A quick guide for every team

Keep it clear.
Keep it GoComet.

Use these simple checks when creating a page, slide, dashboard, or customer message. If you remember one thing: make complex logistics feel clear and connected.

Color & status

✓
DoStart with dark navy and white. Use blue for actions and cyan to highlight one important detail. Label every status.
×
Don’tUse bright colors just for decoration, or make people guess what a color means.

Type & headings

✓
DoUse Lexend for headings and DM Sans for reading. Keep headings short; apply the white-to-ice gradient to one key phrase.
×
Don’tGradient whole paragraphs, use tiny text, or mix unrelated fonts and weights.

Layout & imagery

✓
DoAlign content to a clean grid and leave breathing room. Choose real ports, cargo, and people at work.
×
Don’tCram content edge to edge, use generic office photos, or put text over a busy image.

Data & proof

✓
DoShow the unit, time period, and source for a number. Keep chart labels and colors consistent.
×
Don’tPublish an unverified claim, hide the chart scale, or use color as the only clue.

Icons & AI

✓
DoUse one simple outline-icon style. For an AI Worker, show what it is doing and when a person needs to review.
×
Don’tMix icon styles or imply that an AI action is complete while it is still running.

When in doubt: remove one thing, make the next action obvious, and check that the page still reads clearly on a small screen.
