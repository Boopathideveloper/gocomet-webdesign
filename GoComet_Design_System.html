04 / Grid & spacing

Space for the signal.

A 12-column desktop grid and consistent spacing rhythm keep dense operational interfaces composed and approachable.

12-column responsive grid

Desktop: max content width 1320px, 12 columns, 24px gutters, 56px page padding. Tablet: 8 columns, 24px gutters. Mobile: 4 columns, 16px gutters and 16px page padding. Collapse multi-column cards before text becomes cramped.

Spacing in practice

Use 8px as the everyday rhythm: 8px within controls, 16px inside cards, 24px between related groups, 32px between content blocks, and 56–88px between major sections. Use 4px only for fine optical adjustments.

05 / Image & icon style

Real-world context. Clear signals.

Use authentic logistics imagery and a restrained icon system to make complex operations feel tangible and easy to scan.

Logistics in motion · art direction

Image direction

Choose documentary-style photos of real ports, vessels, containers, warehouses, and people at work. Favor one clear subject, natural perspective, and cool blue daylight. Add a subtle navy overlay only when needed for readable text. Avoid generic office stock, overly polished 3D scenes, heavy filters, or placing copy over busy detail.

Icon direction

Use simple outline icons with rounded caps, consistent 1.75–2px strokes, and a 24px viewBox (16px for compact controls). Use one icon family per interface. Apply cyan or blue only to the active state; pair status icons with a text label.
